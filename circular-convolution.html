🔄 Circular Convolution

Multiplying two DFTs convolves the signals circularly. It's the same reflect-and-slide as ordinary convolution — except the flipped signal is periodically extended, so the tap that slides off one edge re-enters at the other. Watch y[n] = Σ x[m]·h̃[n−m] get built one sample at a time, then zero-pad and watch the wrap turn harmless.
