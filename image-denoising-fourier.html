Periodic noise creates isolated bright peaks in the spectrum. Narrow notches suppress those peaks while retaining almost every other image frequency, so the original can be recovered much more closely.
Teaching caution: Fourier denoising works best when noise is high-frequency or frequency-localized. If noise overlaps useful detail, filtering removes some of both.
