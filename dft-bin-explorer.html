Click a spectrum bin to overlay the real sinusoid that bin (together with its conjugate partner) contributes to x[n]. Low-k bins add slow waves, bins near N/2 add the fastest wiggle.

freq|X[k]| — the N bins

Computed order k = 0 … N−1: positive frequencies climb to N/2, then the folded negative side returns to dc.

positive frequencies (low → high) negative frequencies (high → low) k = 0 (dc) and k = N/2 (±π)
For a real signal, X[N−k] = X*[k]: bin N−k is bin k’s negative-frequency partner, so the magnitude is symmetric about N/2. Hover any bin — its partner lights up too.
How to read N bins: bins 0 → N/2 are positive frequencies from dc upward (low → high); bins N/2 → N are negative frequencies folding back toward dc (high → low). A real lowpass signal therefore peaks at k = 0 and near k = N, and dips at k = N/2. Switch to the centered view to see the fold undone.
