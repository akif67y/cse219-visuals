windWind one period around the origin

Each sample x[n] is placed at radius x[n] and turned by −2πk·n/N; the center of mass of the N points is ak. At an integer k the winding closes after one period and the points sit on the wound continuous curve.

Click any bin in the spectrum to wind at that k. Drag the slider between bins: the loop no longer closes, the mass spreads out, and the centroid drifts — a periodic signal has nothing at those rates. Picture after 3Blue1Brown, But what is the Fourier Transform?
Only N numbers. Sampling one period made the spectrum N-periodic; the DFT is the one period of it we keep, X[k] = N·ak. Bins 0 → N/2 climb from dc to the highest frequency, bins N/2 → N hold the folded negative frequencies — and bin k is simply the harmonic at k·f₀.
