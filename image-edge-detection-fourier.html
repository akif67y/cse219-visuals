Edges correspond to rapid spatial changes. Removing the low-frequency center and keeping the outer spectrum emphasizes boundaries, texture, and other sharp transitions.
Teaching caution: this is not a full modern edge detector like Canny. Fourier high-pass filtering also emphasizes fine texture and noise, so the result is deliberately described as edge-like.
